# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 12, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **DiscordMusicBot** ("the Bot") processes information when you interact with it.

## 1. Information Processed

To provide its functionality, the Bot may receive and temporarily process information supplied through Discord, including:

* Discord user identifiers.
* Discord server and channel information.
* Voice-channel state required to connect the Bot to a voice channel.
* Commands and search queries submitted to the Bot.
* Technical information necessary to execute Discord interactions.

For example, when a user requests a song, the Bot processes the search query in order to locate and play the requested audio.

## 2. Music Searches and Third-Party Services

The Bot may send or process music search requests through third-party services used to locate and retrieve requested content.

DiscordMusicBot currently uses YouTube-related functionality for music searching and retrieval.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 3. Data Storage

The Bot does not currently maintain a database of Discord users or user profiles.

Music files may be downloaded temporarily or locally by the Bot as part of providing audio playback.

Server logs may contain technical information such as command execution, errors, download activity, and other information necessary for operation and debugging.

## 4. Voice Communications

The Bot connects to Discord voice channels for music playback.

DiscordMusicBot is not designed to record, transcribe, or permanently store users' voice conversations.

## 5. Sharing of Information

DiscordMusicBot does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide Bot functionality, including Discord and services involved in locating or retrieving requested media.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the Bot and its configuration.

However, no online service or software system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's Privacy

DiscordMusicBot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies.

The Bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children outside the information necessary to provide Discord interactions.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Bot's functionality, data-processing practices, or legal requirements change.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this document indicates the latest revision.

## 9. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the DiscordMusicBot project's official repository or another contact method published by the developer.
